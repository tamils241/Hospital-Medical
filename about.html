Our Journey

A legacy of trust, care, and continuous innovation.

2000

Foundation

Stackly Hospital opens its doors in Salem with 50 beds and a team of 20 dedicated doctors.

2013

NABH Accreditation

Achieved National Accreditation Board certification, validating our commitment to quality standards.

2016

Expansion to 200 Beds

Major infrastructure expansion adding cardiac care, neurology, and a dedicated ICU wing.

2019

Robotic Surgery Launch

Tamil Nadu's first Da Vinci robotic surgical system installed, revolutionizing minimally invasive care.

2022

Research Institute

Stackly Research Institute inaugurated, focusing on oncology, cardiology, and infectious disease.

2024

300-Bed Expansion

New tower inaugurated, bringing total capacity to 300 beds with 12 state-of-the-art operating theaters.
